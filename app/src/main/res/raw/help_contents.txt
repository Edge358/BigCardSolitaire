<h2>Big Card Solitaire: how to play</h2>
<p>This tablet edition plays draw-three Klondike. Build the four foundations
upward by suit from Ace to King. In the seven working columns, build downward
in alternating red and black. Only a King or a run starting with a King can
fill an empty column.</p>
<ul>
<li><b>Tap to move:</b> tap a face-up card or the first card of a run, then its
legal destination. Gold marks the selection. Tap the selected run or bare
 table space to cancel.</li>
<li><b>Drag:</b> slide a card or run to its destination and lift your finger.</li>
<li><b>Draw:</b> tap the upper-left deck for up to three cards. Only the top
face-up card beside it is playable. Tap the empty deck space to recycle.</li>
<li><b>Hint:</b> cyan outlines show a legal source and destination. Tap again
for another move. Hint can point to the deck, or report no moves available.
It does not move cards or rank strategy.</li>
<li><b>Undo:</b> reverse the last move, draw or recycle. Repeat to go farther back.</li>
<li><b>Crowded stacks:</b> press and hold to expand a movable stack. Scroll
vertically, then tap a card to select its run or drag it horizontally.</li>
<li><b>Fling:</b> a quick fling of one card can send it to a legal foundation.
With default settings, further tableau cards may follow automatically.</li>
<li><b>New Game:</b> immediately replaces the deal. Undo cannot restore the old deal.</li>
<li><b>Exit:</b> saves and closes. Switching apps also saves. Reopening resumes
the saved deal and undo history.</li>
</ul>
<p>There is no hidden game-selection or options menu in this edition.
The illustrated player guide is <b>docs/HOW_TO_PLAY.md</b> in the project.</p>
